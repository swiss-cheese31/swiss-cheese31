I genuinely enjoy working across the whole craft of a machine, from CAD and multiphysics simulation to microfabrication and the slow art of bonding silicon a few microns at a time.
If it has to survive heat, stress, and the void, is meant to *see, fly, or reach orbit*, and is as painstaking to forge as the [Rings of Power](https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Rings_of_Power) in the smithies of [Eregion](https://lotr.fandom.com/wiki/Eregion), I probably want to build it.

> *One does not simply align a copper pillar to the micron... but I do it anyway.*
